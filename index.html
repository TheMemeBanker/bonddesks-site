The maturity ladder

Bonds pay more for longer commitments. So does this desk. Your reward weight = your $BOND stake × your maturity multiplier — and the weekly 961,538 $OTC is split pro-rata across everyone's weighted stake.

01Stake & pick a maturity

Hold $BOND and choose a rung. Higher maturities carry more weight, so they earn a larger slice of every weekly coupon.

02Coupons accrue by the second

Rewards are pro-rata by weighted stake and prorated to the exact time you're in. Weekly snapshots pay out from the published pool wallet.

03Unstake whenever

Principal is never locked. Exit a committed maturity early and you keep every base coupon — you only give up the boost above 1.00×. No penalty on your $BOND, ever.
