How it works

01Hold $BOND

Staking is snapshot-based. Keep $BOND in your wallet — no deposits, no contracts to trust, nothing to un-stake later.

02Weekly snapshot

Every week, holder balances are snapshotted. Your coupon is your share of that week's 961,538 $OTC emission, pro-rata.

03Coupons paid in $OTC

Payouts come from the published pool wallet below — every distribution is a public on-chain transfer you can audit.
