The coupon book

Bonds pay more for longer commitments. So does this desk. Your reward weight = your $BD stake × your maturity multiplier — and each weekly coupon of 961,538 $OTC is split pro-rata across everyone's weighted stake.

1.00×weightCallflexiblestay fully liquid, base coupon
1.25×weightShort Bond3 months+25% coupon for a quarter
1.50×weightNote6 months+50% coupon for a half-year
2.00×weightLong Bond12 monthsdouble coupon — the deepest desk
  1. 01Stake & pick a maturity

    Hold $BD and choose a coupon. Higher maturities carry more weight, so they earn a larger slice of every weekly coupon.

  2. 02Coupons accrue by the second

    Rewards are pro-rata by weighted stake and prorated to the exact time you're in. Weekly snapshots pay out from the published pool wallet.

  3. 03Unstake whenever

    Principal is never locked. Exit a committed maturity early and you keep every base coupon — you only give up the boost above 1.00×. No penalty on your $BD, ever.
